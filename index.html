Biography

Raised in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, Tyler Scherer holds a BFA in Musical Theater from Point Park University. Having performed in over 30 theater productions—including national tours—he transitioned into national commercials (Old Navy, Walmart) before emerging as one of vertical drama's top male leads.
